## Phase 1: Qualification (before MOU)

- [ ] Partner is a legal entity (registered charity, CIO, CIC, Ltd, Trust, etc.)
- [ ] Partner operates at least one physical site where eligible recipients access services
- [ ] Partner has existing nutritional/health support or food provision
- [ ] Partner has staff present during distribution hours
- [ ] Partner has secure, dry storage
- [ ] Partner agrees to anonymised daily tally (no names collected)
- [ ] Partner agrees to AE reporting within 24h
- [ ] Partner has no active safeguarding investigations

**Decision:** Proceed to MOU?  Y / N  
**Reason if N:** ___________________

---

## Phase 2: MOU Execution

- [ ] Partnership Framework template sent to partner
- [ ] Partner reviews, requests amendments (if any)
- [ ] Both parties sign (digital signature acceptable)
- [ ] Signed copy stored by Substrate (private repo directory)
- [ ] Partner receives: signed MOU + current protocol (PROTOCOL/SPEC.md) + AE form + contact card

---

## Phase 3: Training & Setup (1-2 hours, on-site or remote)

- [ ] Substrate lead walks partner staff through:
  - [ ] Product overview (Core 5, optional tiers)
  - [ ] Contraindication quick-reference card
  - [ ] Storage requirements (temperature, humidity)
  - [ ] Daily tally sheet completion
  - [ ] AE form completion + Substrate AE line
  - [ ] Stock reconciliation at month end
- [ ] Partner nominates **one named staff lead** for Substrate communications
- [ ] Emergency contact card exchanged

---

## Phase 4: First Distribution

- [ ] First stock delivery confirmed (packing note + CoA shared)
- [ ] Partner confirms receipt, condition, counts match
- [ ] Go-live date agreed
- [ ] First week: daily check-in (call or message)
- [ ] Weekly thereafter

---

## Phase 5: Ongoing (Monthly)

- [ ] Partner returns tally sheet + unused stock by 5th working day
- [ ] Substrate reconciles, files, publishes aggregate
- [ ] Substrate sends next month's stock (if continuing)
- [ ] Quarterly: partner satisfaction call (15 min)
