## What Substrate provides

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Supplements | Free-of-charge quantities of Substrate Stack products as specified per delivery |
| Protocol | Current dosing and contraindication guidance for each product |
| Lab documentation | Third-party CoA (Certificate of Analysis) for each batch |
| Training | Written guidance on distribution, storage, adverse event reporting |

## What Partner provides

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Distribution point | Safe, dry storage and supervised distribution to eligible recipients |
| Recipient vetting | Criteria consistent with the Partner's existing service eligibility |
| Record-keeping | Anonymised daily uptake counts (how many sachets/units taken each day) |
| Adverse event reporting | Any suspected adverse reaction within 24 hours via Substrate AE form |
| Data return | Monthly anonymised summary to Substrate within 5 working days of month end |

---

## Eligibility — who receives

Supplements are distributed **free of charge** to individuals who are:

1. Experiencing homelessness (rough sleeping, sofa-surfing, B&B/temporary accommodation)
2. In addiction recovery or active harm-reduction engagement
3. Experiencing financial exclusion that prevents independent supplement access
4. Referred by a Partner organisation's own frontline staff on a case-by-case basis
5. [To be inserted: local eligibility criteria specific to this partner's service model]

Partner retains full control over individual eligibility. Substrate does not contract for recipient-level data.

---

## Data and confidentiality

- Partner collects **no names, NHS numbers, or personal identifiers** as part of this arrangement.
- Uptake data is reported as daily totals only (e.g., "50 sachets distributed on 12/09/2026").
- No recipient-level data leaves Partner premises.
- Both parties comply with GDPR and data protection obligations applicable to their respective roles.
- Either party may audit the other's data handling practices on 14 days' notice.

---

## Adverse events

Partner will report any of the following within 24 hours of becoming aware:

- Any person who appears to experience an adverse reaction after taking a Substrate product
- Any product found to be damaged, contaminated, or out of date on delivery
- Any significant disruption to storage conditions (flood, temperature failure, etc.)

Substrate will respond within 48 hours. Escalation path: see `OPERATIONS/adverse_event_reporting.md`.

---

## Duration and termination

- Term: 12 months from execution date.
- Either party may terminate on 14 days' written notice.
- On termination, Partner shall return or safely dispose of any undistributed stock and submit final uptake data.

---

## Liability

- Partner is not liable for adverse effects arising from correct use of products per the protocol.
- Substrate is not liable for Partner's storage, distribution, or eligibility decisions beyond the scope of this agreement.
- This agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship.
