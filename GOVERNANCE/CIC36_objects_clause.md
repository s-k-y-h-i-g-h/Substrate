# CIC36 Objects Clause — Substrate CIC

Copy verbatim into Companies House CIC36 form field "Statement of the company's objects."

---

The objects of the company are:

**(a) Poverty relief and health advancement.**

To relieve poverty and advance health by providing evidence-based nutritional, longevity, and cognitive supplements — including vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and nootropic compounds — to people experiencing homelessness, addiction recovery, financial exclusion, or other forms of socioeconomic disadvantage, distributed through partner organisations already embedded in those communities.

**(b) Public education and health literacy.**

To advance public health by making evidence-based knowledge of nutrition, biohacking, and longevity interventions freely available to the general public — including, without limitation or restriction of audience, to people who are not members of any particular group and who may not be in clinical care — by publishing, in plain language, what the evidence supports, what it does not, together with dosage standards, contraindications, interaction warnings, and the full reasoning behind every claim published by the company.

**(c) Open knowledge.**

To publish open protocols, dosage standards, sourcing specifications, procurement guidelines, and health outcome data derived from the distribution activities described in (a), for the benefit of the public and the public health research community, without cost or restrictive licensing.

**(d) Research and standards.**

To commission, conduct, and disseminate independent research into cost-effective nutritional and pharmacological interventions for populations with limited access to conventional healthcare, and to develop replicable distribution standards that other organisations may adopt without restriction.

**(e) Ancillary powers.**

To do all such other lawful things as are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects, including but not limited to fundraising, grant-making, procurement, logistics, partnership management, and the provision of materials and guidance free of charge to the public.

---

## Notes for filing

- Objects (a) satisfies the "community interest" test: it is a charitable purpose (poverty relief + health advancement) even though filed under CIC rather than CIO.
- Objects (b) is the public-education limb and is deliberately broad on audience. It is written to cover
  an unrestricted general public rather than a defined beneficiary group, because the company's educational
  purpose is not tied to eligibility. **This wording has not been reviewed by the CIC Regulator and is the
  single most likely clause to draw a query** — see the flag below.
- Objects (c) prevents the protocol from becoming proprietary IP — this is the open-source guarantee baked
  into the legal structure.
- Objects (d) positions Substrate as a standards body, not just a distributor.
- Objects (e) is standard CIC boilerplate and covers all operational activity, including the free
  provision of materials to the public.

## ⚠️ Filing flag — read before submitting

The objects clause has been **restructured** to add a public-education limb (b) and to renumber what were
(b), (c) and (d). Any prior rejection of a CIC application may have been caused by clause wording, and
renumbering compounds that risk. Before filing:

1. Confirm with the CIC Regulator (`cicregulator@companieshouse.gov.uk` / 029 2150 7420) that a general
   public-education object is acceptable alongside a poverty-relief object, and in what form.
2. Confirm whether a CIC whose objects serve an undefined general public still satisfies the
   community-interest test without a defined community in the articles. **This is the load-bearing
   question in the whole filing.** If it fails, the mitigation is to define "community" in the articles
   as "the people of the United Kingdom" rather than to narrow the objects clause.
3. Confirm the prior rejection reason. No record of it is held in this repository, and re-filing blind
   repeats the cycle.
