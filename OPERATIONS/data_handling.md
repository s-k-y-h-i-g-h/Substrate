# Data Handling Policy — Substrate

**Scope:** All operational data held by Substrate CIC and partner distribution organisations.

---

## Principles

1. **Privacy by design.** We collect the minimum data necessary. If we don't need a name, we don't ask for it.
2. **Anonymised first.** Daily aggregate counts only. No individual health data leaves partner premises unless legally compelled.
3. **No re-identification.** We do not hold, and will not request, any data that could be used to identify recipients (names, addresses, NHS numbers, dates of birth, postcodes).
4. **Open non-personal data.** All non-personal operational data is published open by default (CC-BY-4.0).
5. **Retention then destruction.** Personal data has a fixed shelf life. We delete it, not archive it.

---

| Data | Holder | Format | Retention | Published? |

---

## GDPR compliance

- **Controller:** Substrate CIC is data controller for data it holds directly.
- **Processor:** Partner distribution organisations are data controllers for any data they hold locally; Substrate is a processor only when receiving data from them.
- **Lawful basis:** Processing is under **Art. 6(1)(e)** — public task / legitimate interest (poverty relief, health advancement). For financial processing: **Art. 6(1)(b)** — contract performance.
- **Data Protection Officer:** Not required for CIC at this scale; review when staff count exceeds 10.
- **ICO registration:** Required once CIC is active and processing personal data (even just partner contact details).
- **Subject access requests:** Substrate will respond within 30 days. Requests must be directed to [substrate-board@substrate.org.uk — placeholder].

---

## Data sharing with partners

Partners receive:
- Only protocol and supply schedule data (non-personal)
- No recipient-level data from any other partner site
- No aggregated data from other partners unless explicitly agreed in partner agreement

---

## Publication commitment

Within 90 days of each quarter end, Substrate will publish:

- Total units distributed (all sites, aggregate)
- Batch numbers currently in circulation
- Adverse event summary (de-identified, by batch)
- Procurement spend summary (total spend, per ingredient, source country)
- Uptake rates by site (raw counts, no individual data)

Published to: `github.com/s-k-y-h-i-g-h/Substrate / data/` directory  
License: CC-BY-4.0 or Open Government Licence equivalent

---

## Incident response

**Data breach:** Substrate will notify affected partner(s) within 72 hours of discovery. No individual recipient data is held by Substrate; partner breach is partner's responsibility under GDPR.

**Police / legal request for data:** Substrate holds no recipient-level data. Any such request will be referred to a solicitor and not acted on without legal advice.
