# Adverse Event Reporting Procedure — Substrate

Applies to: all distribution partner sites, all Substrate team members.

---

## What counts as an adverse event

Any of the following:

1. Any physical or psychological reaction that the recipient attributes to a Substrate product
2. Product found damaged, contaminated, or past expiry on delivery
3. Any disruption to storage conditions (flood, temperature failure, pest infestation)
4. Recipient reports unfamiliar or concerning symptoms after taking product, regardless of suspected cause

**Does NOT require confirmed causation.** If the recipient thinks it's related, it gets reported. We adjudicate later.

---

## Severity

| Tier | Examples | Required action |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Minor** | Mild GI upset, transient headache, sleep disruption | Record on AE form; report to Substrate within 5 days |
| **Moderate** | Persistent symptoms (>24h), new rash, anxiety episode | Record on AE form; report to Substrate within 24h; consider NHS 111 advice |
| **Severe** | Hospitalisation, anaphylaxis, suspected overdose | Record on AE form; partner calls 111/999 + Substrate simultaneously; preserve product batch |

## Substrate response

| SLA | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Within 24h** | Substrate director acknowledges receipt |
| **Within 48h** | Assessment: product-related? batch issue? contraindication known? |
| **Within 1 week** | Decision: hold batch, recall, adjust protocol, or clear for continued use |
| **Ongoing** | Events logged in private AE register; summary reported in annual accounts |

---

## Escalation criteria

Substrate initiates full recall / suspension of ingredient if:
- Two or more moderate events from the **same batch** within 30 days of distribution
- Any severe event confirmed or strongly suspected product-related
- MHRA/EMA/FDA safety alert on any ingredient currently in distribution

---

## Record retention

- AE forms retained for 6 years (aligned with CIC annual reporting cycle)
- Stored at partner site; Substrate retains executive summary only
- Personal identifiers (names, NHS numbers) are not accepted or retained
