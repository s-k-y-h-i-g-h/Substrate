# Distribution Protocol — Substrate

## Pre-distribution checklist

- [ ] All batch CoAs reviewed and filed in `FINANCE/procurement/` (physical or digital)
- [ ] Expiry dates confirmed (>12 months remaining)
- [ ] Packaging labelled: product name, batch number, expiry, "Not medical advice", partner contact, Substrate AE line
- [ ] Inventory count logged before packing
- [ ] Partner storage conditions confirmed (cool, dry, pest-free)

## Packing

- Core 5: daily sachets in labelled 30-day sleeves (10 per week strip or box)
- Longevity/Cognitive: individual strips per ingredient
- Pallet/box: partner name, date of pack, total units, batch reference

## Transport

- Local UK: Royal Mail Tracked 48 or courier; temperature-controlled vehicle not required for shelf-stable products
- Label packages "Do not freeze / Do not expose to temperatures above 25C"
- Goods in transit insured (CIC policy covers distribution)

## At partner site

- Partner staff confirm receipt against packing note
- Check for visible damage
- Store in designated area (locked if necessary)
- Acknowledge receipt via signed delivery note (Substrate retains; partner gets copy)

## Onward distribution to recipients

- Partner follows its own eligibility criteria per Partnership Agreement
- Substrate materials distributed in sealed sachets only
- No Substrate product to be re-sold or given to persons outside eligible population without Substrate written consent

## Stock reconciliation

At month end:
- Partner returns unused stock + confirmed daily tally
- Substrate reconciles against issued quantities
- Discrepancies >5% flagged for investigation

## Cold chain / special handling

Current formulation is shelf-stable. If liquid or temperature-sensitive formulations are added to protocol in future, cold chain requirements will be specified as a protocol revision per `PROTOCOL/SPEC.md` version bump.
